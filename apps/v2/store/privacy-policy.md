# Check-ins Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

Check-ins helps people track long-term projects, tasks, and when they last worked on those projects.

## Data We Collect

- Account email address, used for sign-in and account recovery.
- Project, task, check-in, reminder, and settings data that you enter into the app.
- Basic authentication/session data handled by Supabase.

## Data We Do Not Collect

- Payment data. Check-ins has no payments.
- Advertising data. Check-ins has no ads.
- Data for third-party ad targeting.

## How Data Is Used

Data is used only to provide the app: account access, syncing, project tracking, task tracking, check-ins, and reminders.

## Data Sharing

Check-ins uses Supabase to store and authenticate user-owned data. Data is not sold and is not shared with ad networks.

## Notifications

Mobile reminders use local notifications after permission is granted. Web reminders only work while the web app is open unless a future service-worker/PWA system is added.

## Account Deletion

Users can delete their account from Settings. Deleting an account removes the auth user and synced Check-ins data.

## Contact

Use the support link in the app or open an issue at https://github.com/Chrissyuh/check-ins/issues.
